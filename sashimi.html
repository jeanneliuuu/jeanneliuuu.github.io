MY ROLE

Sustainabli 🌱 is a startup focused on providing B2B solutions for labs to enhance sustainability and reduce energy waste through innovative technology.

During my time at Sustainabli, I was their sole product designer and led all aspects of design from defining the design system to launching new product initiatives. In addition to spearheading design efforts, I collaborated closely with cross-functional teams to ensure alignment between design, engineering, and CEOs, resulting in streamlined processes and successful project delivery.

Challenge

How might we design a more intuitive and engaging interface for research organizations to easily monitor and manage fume hoods?

At Sustainabli, we focused on a key question to shape our product vision for Sashimi: how could we best support research organizations in managing lab resources efficiently while minimizing energy waste? By centering the needs of these stakeholders, we designed an intuitive and engaging interface, enabling researchers to seamlessly monitor and control fume hoods with ease.

Research

Understanding where energy was quietly wasted

Conversations with lab managers and researchers surfaced a pattern: fume hoods were routinely left open overnight with no one accountable, there was no single place to see sash status or usage across labs, and admins had no easy way to track efficiency or reward the labs doing it well. That gap directly shaped the overview dashboard, proactive alerts, and the lab competitions built later.

Approach

Turning research into design decisions

1

Surface status at a glance

Sash height, energy, and cost sit on one overview so a lab manager never has to dig for the number that matters.

2

Make monitoring proactive, not reactive

Alerts flag a hood left open or an efficiency drop before it turns into wasted energy, instead of waiting for someone to notice.

3

Keep setup and access low-friction

Adding labs, rooms, and people takes a couple of guided steps, so onboarding a whole institution doesn't become its own project.

Final Designs

Streamline your lab's energy usage

Empower your research labs with intuitive monitoring and actionable insights, optimizing fume hood usage to enhance safety, sustainability, and overall performance.

Overview

Centralized digital dashboard

Enables users to control all fume hoods from one intuitive interface. Users can access from a centralized platform to monitor and adjust multiple hoods, streamlining lab operations and saving time.

Real-time status updates for all connected fume hoods, customizable alerts for unusual energy consumption patterns and data visualization for trend analysis and reporting.

People

Easily invite and add labs, streamlining the process of expanding Sashimi's usage within an institution.

Performance Tracking: By providing an overview of each lab's performance, including average sash height and overall efficiency grade, users can quickly identify top performers and areas needing improvement.

Fume Hoods

Ensuring safe and efficient lab environments

Offers users a quick overview of lab spaces, providing detailed room information on fume hood availability and quantity to enhance resource management and safety compliance across the research facility.

Users can receive immediate notifications about unusual energy consumption patterns or potential inefficiencies in fume hood usage and provide tailored recommendations for improving energy efficiency.

Data Query

Unlocking energy insights with customizable data queries.

By allowing users to run comparisons between different labs or groups, this feature enables them to benchmark performance, identify best practices, track progress toward sustainability goals, and pinpoint areas for improvement.

An important feature is making sure the user can compare data across all labs. By facilitating these comparisons, users gain valuable insights that drive informed decision-making and encourage continuous improvement in energy management practices.

Shut the Sash

Community Building

By incorporating competitions into Sashimi, it allows users to engage in friendly challenges that promote energy-saving behaviors and track their progress in reducing energy waste.

With interactive graphs, users can more easily visualize and quantify their cost savings.

Results

Rolled out across research labs

Sashimi shipped as the primary way labs monitor fume hoods and track energy impact, with the overview dashboard, people/lab management, data queries, and Shut the Sash competitions launching as one coherent system.

Takeaways

Learnings

1
Great products are shaped through tradeoffs

Working on Sustainabli reinforced that successful product design isn't about finding perfect solutions, it's about making thoughtful tradeoffs between user needs, business priorities, and technical constraints. The strongest outcomes came from experimenting quickly, aligning cross-functional perspectives, and making informed decisions with incomplete information.

2
Complexity is a design opportunity

Designing for scientific workflows strengthened my ability to transform highly technical systems into experiences that feel intuitive without sacrificing depth. Rather than simplifying the product itself, the goal was to simplify how people interact with it.
